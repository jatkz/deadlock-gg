Calculation coverage

Results use the cached asset snapshot, not a live game simulation. Ability conditions, stacks, procs, alternate fire, and chained effects need explicit inputs or additional formulas. Unsupported scaling is shown without a damage result.

Formulas and sources

Bullet base = base bullet damage × (1 + boons × boon growth) + supported hero spirit scaling. Raw bullet = bullet base × (1 + weapon bonus / 100) + flat bonus.

Ability component = upgraded base + spirit × upgraded spirit coefficient. Damage taken = raw damage × (1 − (resistance − reduction) / 100) × (1 + amplification / 100). Barrier absorbs damage before health.

Shop bonuses use the highest reached investment threshold per category, without adding legacy tier bonuses. Display rounding happens only after calculation.

Health = (boon-scaled base health × (1 + vitality investment and base-health bonuses) + flat health) × max-health modifiers. Melee uses boon growth, half the weapon damage bonus, and additive melee bonuses. Heavy melee boon growth follows the hero’s base heavy/light ratio.

Ammo = (base magazine + flat ammo bonuses + supported spirit scaling) × (1 + total ammo percentage bonuses), rounded up. Fire rate includes supported item and hero spirit bonuses; it excludes reloads and conditional buffs. Shotgun pellets do not multiply shots per second.
